## 8. Risks and Dependencies
Identifying potential risks and dependencies early can help mitigate issues.

*   **Missing Documents:** Delay in obtaining official transcripts or letters of recommendation can jeopardize applications. **Dependency:** Timely request and receipt of academic records/references.
*   **Unverified Eligibility Rules:** Applying without thoroughly checking specific eligibility criteria (e.g., minimum GPA, specific course prerequisites, visa sponsorship requirements) can lead to wasted effort or immediate rejection. **Dependency:** Thorough review of each job description's requirements.
*   **Application Deadlines:** Missing application deadlines is a critical risk, especially for competitive roles. **Dependency:** Proactive tracking of all deadlines and submitting applications well in advance.
*   **Generic Application Materials:** Submitting a non-tailored resume or cover letter for highly specific AI/ML roles can significantly reduce chances of selection. **Dependency:** Dedicated effort to customize materials for each application.
*   **Lack of Interview Preparation:** Inadequate preparation for technical and behavioral interviews specific to AI/ML roles. **Dependency:** Consistent practice and mock interviews.
*   **Limited Opportunities:** The number of suitable AI/ML internships might vary, requiring broader search strategies. **Dependency:** Continuous and diversified search for opportunities.
*   **Technology Stack Mismatch:** If the student's current technical skills don't perfectly align with preferred roles, additional learning might be required. **Dependency:** Self-study or online course completion for critical skill gaps.
